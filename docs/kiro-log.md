### Decisions and limitations
- `float` features are computed in double and narrowed once; `Math.Log` can differ in the last double bit across CPU architectures, so only the integers and the PPM score are hashed or compared.
- `InferenceEvidenceV1` requires `ScreeningFactSeq == AsOfFactSeq + 1`; 8.3 may amend this before first persistence via a new `FormatVersion`.
- Destination accounts are not purged (they are not features). Full PaySim has not been run, so the purge's effect on real data (it may leave a split class-empty and then `Run` throws) is unmeasured.
- Fixture metrics will only be fixture metrics. Nothing here trains a model; 8.2 owns that.
