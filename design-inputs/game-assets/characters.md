# Characters

## Visual reference and roster

The visual reference is Kenney's **Shape Characters** sprite sheet: softly shaded, colorful 2D geometric heads with simple, readable eyes, eyebrows, and mouths. The four player characters consist of **heads only**, with **no hands, arms, bodies, or legs**. Their full shapes stay recognizable rather than being redrawn as overhead views.

| Player | Shape | Color | Geometry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1** | Circle | Orange | Fully round, smooth circular head. |
| **P2** | Square | Pink | Nearly straight sides and four lightly softened corners. |
| **P3** | Diamond | Yellow | Four-sided diamond with points at the top, right, bottom, and left. |
| **P4** | Rounded square | Green | Four-sided head with distinctly broad, rounded corners; softer silhouette than P2. |

Each character uses the same facial-expression vocabulary. Shape and color remain constant across expression states.

## Facial expression states

Facial details follow the reference sheet's simple visual language: bold dark eyebrows, white eyes with dark pupils, curved or angled eyelids, and small uncluttered mouths. Expressions are identical in meaning across all four characters.

### Baseline / initial neutral

A deliberately plain, unexcited face: relaxed open eyes, level or nearly level eyebrows, and a short, flat mouth with no smile. The character looks calm but somewhat dull—not cheerful. This is the initial neutral appearance and also the visual character of the **2/4 power** face.

### Movement direction — eye gaze only

During movement, the pupils look **left, right, up, or down** according to the player's current movement direction. The head shape and face orientation stay fixed; only the gaze direction changes. This is a continuous directional cue, **not a new emotional expression**, and it is unrelated to the direction in which candles appear or land.

### Power-level expressions

Each player starts at **1/4 power**; each candle collected adds **+1**. The four power levels correspond to four progressively happier faces:

| Power | Expression | Exact facial appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1/4** | **Frown** | Slightly worried or disappointed eyes; eyebrows slant subtly upward toward the center; a small, clearly downward-curving mouth. Unhappy, but not crying or panicked. |
| **2/4** | **Idle / neutral** | Ordinary open eyes and relaxed, mostly level eyebrows; a small, flat mouth. Emotionally neutral, with no smile. |
| **3/4** | **Happy smile** | Open, brightened eyes; relaxed or slightly lifted brows; a distinct upward-curving smile. Happy, but restrained. |
| **4/4** | **Super happy / best smile** | Wide, lively eyes with visibly lifted, cheerful brows; a broader, more pronounced upward-curving grin than 3/4. This is the strongest ordinary happiness state. |

The eye pupils can still convey movement direction in these normal power-level faces.

### Full-power charge release

**When a player at 4/4 releases their charge:** a single intense **energy-release / screaming** face. Both eyes are squeezed firmly shut into tight curved creases; brows are tensed and drawn inward; the mouth is wide open in a simple dark oval, as if yelling with exertion. The expression communicates a forceful release of energy, not happiness.

### Blast hit reaction

**When a player is actually hit by the released blast:** a **2-second override** of that player's current expression. Both eyes become clear **X shapes**, with brows knocked out of their usual relaxed alignment and a short, crooked, pained mouth. The look is stunned, temporarily knocked-out, and cartoonish—not graphic. Only players hit by the blast receive this face.

### Unequal-power collision reaction

**When a lower-power player clashes with a higher-power player and the defined impact occurs:** the **lower-power player only** displays a **2-second override** of their current expression. Eyes open wide with small pupils; eyebrows raise and angle upward toward the center; the mouth becomes a small open, alarmed oval or tight worried curve. This reads as startled, scared, and impacted. The higher-power player has **no impact-expression change**.

### Winner

**For the player with the highest score:** one **victory expression**. The eyes turn into strongly raised, joyful closed-eye arcs, the brows lift, and the mouth forms an unmistakably wide, triumphant smile. Unlike the 4/4 power grin, this expression conveys celebration of winning rather than simply being very happy. No additional winner or loser expressions are defined.

### Ghost — expression only

The ghost has **one permanent facial expression** throughout gameplay: an overtly **evil / mean** look, with sharply inward-slanted, heavy eyebrows; narrowed, glaring eyes; and a small crooked, malicious smirk. **No expression changes** occur for the ghost. No other ghost characteristics are defined here.

## Ground shadow

Each player has a **minor, soft, faint shadow** directly beneath the head, with a slight offset toward the **bottom-right**. The apparent light comes diagonally from the **top-left**, rather than from directly above the camera. The shadow is subtle and serves only to ground the character visually; it is not large or dramatic.

No other animation details are defined in this document.
